# Hyperfine structure of the 4f²6s ⁴P J = 1/2 level (IDEN2 row 1093)

Status, 2026-09-17: **found, not inserted.** The level is fixed in IDEN2 at its
lower hyperfine component, 49652.297 cm⁻¹, with the matching lines assigned
there. It is not in `new_levels.txt` and has no ledger rows. No measured
wavenumber in `Pr3_lines.xlsx`, `dlv.dat` or Sugar's line list has been
changed. It stays like this until the unknown levels with strong predicted
transitions to it (rows 910, 915, 921, 922, 949 and a few others) have been
found, or given up on.

Its companion is `4f26s_2P_0.5_hfs.md`, row 1089, which is the same pair of
basis states with the weights exchanged (85 % (1,1/2), 14 % (0,1/2)) and which
has A ≈ −0.157.

## Terms

* **Level 1093.** The row of `IDEN2/enlev.dat` labelled `f26s _3P4P`, J = 1/2,
  calculated at 49610.2 cm⁻¹. It has no pipeline identifier yet, so
  `IDEN2/IDEN_level_ids.txt` has no line for it.
* **Composition** (`levels_pub.xlsx`): 84 % 4f²(³P₀)6s (0,1/2) and
  14 % 4f²(³P₁)6s (1,1/2). The brackets name the 4f² core state and the
  (core J, 6s j) pair of the coupling.
* **Hyperfine structure (hfs).** The ¹⁴¹Pr nucleus has spin I = 5/2. A level
  of total electronic angular momentum J couples to it to total angular
  momentum F = |J − I| … J + I. For J = 1/2 there are two sublevels, F = 2 and
  F = 3.
* **A.** The magnetic-dipole hyperfine constant of a level, in cm⁻¹. A
  sublevel lies at `E(F) = E_c + (A/2)·[F(F+1) − I(I+1) − J(J+1)]`. For
  J = 1/2 that puts F = 3 at E_c + 1.25 A and F = 2 at E_c − 1.75 A. The
  splitting is 3A, and F = 3 lies above F = 2 when A > 0.
* **Centre of gravity (c.g.), E_c.** The mean of the sublevel energies weighted
  by their degeneracies 2F + 1, here (7·E(F=3) + 5·E(F=2)) / 12. It is the
  energy a level list should give.
* **σ.** A line's wavenumber in cm⁻¹. Every line discussed here has 1093 as its
  **lower** level, so σ = E_upper − E_1093. The component to the higher
  sublevel, F = 3, therefore has the **smaller** wavenumber.
* **7 : 5.** When the upper level's own hfs is not resolved, the two
  components of a line have intensities in the ratio of the lower sublevels'
  degeneracies: F = 3 : F = 2 = 7 : 5.
* **θ₁, θ₂, a_4f, a_6s.** In Reader & Sugar (1965), a 4f²6s level has
  `A = θ₁·a_4f + θ₂·a_6s`. θ₁ and θ₂ are angular coefficients computed in
  intermediate coupling. a_4f = 0.029 ± 0.003 cm⁻¹ and
  a_6s = 0.639 ± 0.002 cm⁻¹ are the radial parameters Reader & Sugar fitted to
  the configuration (see `Work_on_hfs_plan.md`).

## 1. How it was found

`level_positions.py --unknown 1093` reports two "firm" positions 1.52 cm⁻¹
apart:

| position (cm⁻¹) | ln R | n_match |
|---|---|---|
| 49653.817 | 30.1 | 7 |
| 49652.297 | 27.7 | 7 |

Both are real, but neither is a level. The script scores every energy as a
level without structure, so it finds each hyperfine sublevel as a separate
position and has no means of joining them.

Evidence that the two positions are the F = 3 and F = 2 sublevels of one level:

1. **Paired lines.** Through four different upper levels, both components of
   the transition are recorded and unassigned, at almost the same separation
   and with comparable intensities. Upper energies are the adopted values in
   `enlev.dat`. I is `Icor` of `Pr3_lines.xlsx`, and unc is its `unc_own`.

   | upper (row, label) | σ, F = 3 (I, char) | σ, F = 2 (I, char) | separation | unc | E_c from pair |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 964 f26p ~3P2S, J = 1/2 | 29724.6026 (16968) | 29726.1177 (14858) | 1.515 | 0.027 | 49653.154 |
   | 962 f26p ~3P4D, J = 1/2 | 30088.5807 (9975) | 30090.1394 (9982) | 1.558 | 0.028 | 49653.166 |
   | 952 f26p ~3P4P, J = 3/2 | 33372.1081 (29302, w) | 33373.5622 (29291, w) | 1.454 | 0.064 | 49653.191 |
   | 1000 f5d2 ~3F4S, J = 3/2 | 21847.1970 (100) | 21848.7330 (50, w) | 1.536 | 0.015 / 0.028 | 49653.177 |

   The upper levels differ, so a common separation must belong to the lower
   level. There are about 0.09 recorded lines per cm⁻¹ in this region, so a
   chance partner within ±0.05 cm⁻¹ has a probability of about 0.01. Four such
   partners by chance would have a probability of about 10⁻⁷. The scatter of
   the four separations (s.d. 0.045) is what line uncertainties of 0.03–0.06
   give.

2. **Single components.** Six more transitions show one component only, and
   they divide evenly between the two sublevels. A level placed at either
   sublevel would leave three good lines unexplained.

   | upper | σ | I | sublevel | E(F) | E_c = E(F=3) − 1.25A or E(F=2) + 1.75A |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 957 f26p ~3P4S | 31243.9493 | 7062 | F = 3 | 49653.857 | 49653.225 |
   | 939 f26p ~3P2P | 35150.3212 | 2950 | F = 3 | 49653.814 | 49653.182 |
   | 972 f26p ~1D2P | 28658.9908 | 4669 | F = 3 | 49653.872 | 49653.240 |
   | 953 f5d2 ~1G2D | 32840.1314 | 25442 | F = 2 | 49652.388 | 49653.272 |
   | 960 f26p ~3P4D | 30511.6182 | 2920 | F = 2 | 49652.401 | 49653.285 |
   | 943 f26p ~3P2D | 34757.5489 | 1856, w | F = 2 | 49652.359 | 49653.243 |

   In IDEN2, 34757.5489 is assigned only to 1093–943. The pipeline lists it as
   a candidate for 059003.000251–059003.000334 (predicted I 17), but that
   assignment is not accepted.

3. **Intensities.** The F = 3 : F = 2 ratios of the pairs are 1.14, 1.00, 1.00
   and 2.0. None falls below 1, which is weakly consistent with 7 : 5 and so
   with A > 0. Photographic intensities cannot establish the sign on their
   own. Section 2 does.

4. **Weak evidence.** 38701.860 (I 11944) matches a transition down to row
   1245 (4f³ ~4S4S) from the F = 2 position. It is predicted at 1194, ten times
   fainter than observed. It is not used in the estimates below.

The upper levels' own A constants (`A_hfs_levels.csv`, computed from
composition) are small: +0.087 (964), −0.008 (962), +0.014 (952). They can
shift a component by a few hundredths of a cm⁻¹ at most, which is within the
scatter above.

## 2. The hyperfine constant

**Measured.** The mean separation of the four pairs is 3A = 1.516 ± 0.022 cm⁻¹
(± is the standard error of the mean), so

    A(1093) = +0.505 ± 0.007 cm⁻¹.

The sign follows from the fact that the component with the smaller
wavenumber, which the level at the higher energy produces, is the stronger
one (section 1, item 3), and from the calculation below.

**Calculated.** Reader & Sugar (1965), intermediate coupling, J = 1/2:

| level | θ₁ | θ₂ | A = θ₁·a_4f + θ₂·a_6s |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4f²(³P₀)6s J = 1/2 (this level) | −0.33 | +0.7972 | **+0.4998** |
| 4f²(³P₁)6s J = 1/2 | +0.33 | −0.1381 | −0.0787 |

* The propagated uncertainty from a_4f and a_6s is ±0.002 cm⁻¹. The method's
  rms over the 22 measured 4f²6s constants is 0.0048 cm⁻¹ (see
  `Work_on_hfs_plan.md` section 2.3). So A_calc = +0.500 ± 0.005 cm⁻¹, which
  agrees with the measured +0.505 ± 0.007 to 0.6 σ.
* The ³P₁-core J = 1/2 level is the known level 059003.000190. Its published
  A = −0.0787 ± 0.0022 equals the θ value, which confirms that the two θ rows
  belong to the pair of J = 1/2 levels as labelled.

**The composition check.** A 6s electron coupled to a core of angular momentum
J₁ contributes a_6s·[J(J+1) + 3/4 − J₁(J₁+1)] / [2J(J+1)] to A. For J = 1/2 that
is +1·a_6s when J₁ = 0 and −1/3·a_6s when J₁ = 1. The 6s operator does not act
on the core, and the J₁ = 0 and J₁ = 1 core states are orthogonal, so the two
parts do not interfere in θ₂.
With a fraction x of J₁ = 0 cores:

    θ₂ = x − (1 − x)/3.

* θ₂ = +0.7972 gives x = 0.848.
* θ₂ = −0.1381 for the other level gives x = 0.146.

Both agree with the 84 % / 14 % of `levels_pub.xlsx`. Taken literally, 84 / 16
gives θ₂ = 0.787 and A = +0.493, and 86 / 14 gives θ₂ = 0.813 and A = +0.510.
The measured A lies inside that range. θ₁ contributes only ∓0.010 cm⁻¹ and is
taken from Reader & Sugar unchanged.

(49652.297), where the level now sits in IDEN2, is F = 2.

## 4. Use in the search for the missing levels

Rows still unknown with strong predicted transitions to 1093 (E_calc from
`enlev.dat`; all of them lie above 1093):

| row | label | J | E_calc | σ ≈ E_calc − 49653.2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 910 | f5d6s ~3P4P | 3/2 | 93427.9 | 43774.7 |
| 915 | f5d6s ~1D2D | 3/2 | 91039.2 | 41386.0 |
| 921 | f5d6s ~3D4D | 1/2 | 89672.3 | 40019.1 |
| 922 | f5d6s ~3D4D | 3/2 | 89509.8 | 39856.6 |
| 949 | f26p ~3P4P | 1/2 | 83488.4 | 33835.2 |

The predicted σ carries the whole uncertainty of the upper level's calculated
energy, so it is only a guide to the region.

A true transition of such a level to 1093 should appear as:

* **a doublet 1.52 ± 0.05 cm⁻¹ wide**. The component with the smaller
  wavenumber (to F = 3) is the stronger, by about 7 : 5, and both are
  recorded. As in section 1, the pair may be tagged `w`, and sometimes only one
  member is recorded.
* **on the IDEN2 scale as it now stands**, with 1093 at its F = 2 sublevel: the
  F = 2 component at the Ritz wavenumber and the F = 3 component 1.52 cm⁻¹
  **lower**. A candidate upper energy is therefore confirmed if the line at
  E_upper − 49652.30 has a partner at E_upper − 49653.82.
* Each of the missing upper levels should show the same doublet in its
  transition to 1093, while its transitions to other levels are single. For
  two candidate positions of a missing level that score similarly, this test
  can decide.
* **A caveat for the f5d6s levels.** They also contain a 6s electron, so their
  own A can be of the same size (several tenths of a cm⁻¹, of either sign),
  unlike the f26p upper levels of section 1. Their lines to 1093 may then show
  more than two components, or a doublet whose spacing departs from 1.52. The
  7 : 5 rule holds only for the sum over the upper sublevels. A candidate
  should not be rejected for a spacing different from 1.52 alone. It should
  be checked for the same pattern in its other lines to 6s-containing levels.
  The f26p row 949 should show the clean doublet.

## 5. Search for row 949 (2026-09-17)

Row 949 is f26p ~3P4P, J = 1/2, E_calc 83488.4. The rms of E_obs − E_calc for
f26p levels is 110 cm⁻¹. Its transition to 1093 has gA = 5.45·10⁷ s⁻¹ (±70 %).
On the intensity scale of its other lines, that gives I ≈ 5400 in total:
about 3150 for the F = 3 component and 2250 for F = 2.

**Method.**

1. `level_positions.py --unknown 949` scanned 83158–83818 cm⁻¹ with every
   calculated transition of 949 except the one to 1093. 1093 is not a level
   of the pipeline, so its transition is dropped. The test below is therefore
   independent of the scan.
2. The same window, shifted down by 49652.3, was searched for every pair of
   recorded lines 1.42–1.62 cm⁻¹ apart.

**The scan.** One position is `firm`: **83426.17**, ln R = +14.4. ln R is the
log of how much more likely the recorded lines are with a level there than
without one. The next best position scores +3.7.

* **The F = 2 reading of 33772.320** would put the level at 83424.62, which is

## 6. The hyperfine constants of rows 964 and 972 (2026-09-17)

Rows 964 (4f²6p ³P ²S, 79378.39) and 972 (4f²6p ¹D ²P, 78312.86) are J = 1/2
levels. Each has two hfs sublevels, F′ = 3 and F′ = 2, 3A′ apart. A′ is the
upper level's hfs constant and A the constant of 1093. The line from F′ to F
lies at σ = X + c(F′)A′ − c(F)A, where c(3) = +1.25, c(2) = −1.75 and X is the
difference of the centres of gravity. For J = 1/2 → 1/2 with I = 5/2, the
relative line strengths (2F+1)(2F′+1){F F′ 1; ½ ½ 5/2}² are:

| F′ → F | 3 → 3 | 3 → 2 | 2 → 3 | 2 → 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| strength | 28 | 35 | 35 | 10 |

**1093–964: four lines.**

| σ_obs | I_obs | reading (A′ > 0) | strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29726.118 | 14858 | 3 → 2 | 35 |
| 29725.817 | 12733 | 2 → 2 | 10 (blend: also accepted as 269–330) |
| 29724.603 | 16968 | 3 → 3 | 28 |
| 29724.400 | 14845 | 2 → 3 | 35 |

* 3A = 1.515 and 1.417, mean 1.466, so A(1093) = +0.489 ± 0.009. The value from
  §2 is +0.505 ± 0.007; the two differ by 1.4σ.
* 3A′ = 0.301 and 0.203, mean 0.252, so **A′(964) = +0.084 ± 0.009**. Four lines
  give three constants, so one closure test is left: σ(3→3) + σ(2→2) should
  equal σ(3→2) + σ(2→3). It misses by 0.098, which is 1.8σ for line
  uncertainties of 0.027.
* The sign comes from the intensities. With A′ < 0 the order reverses:
  29726.118 would be 2 → 2, the weakest component, and 29725.817 would be 3 → 2,
  one of the strongest. The two outer lines have equal intensity, which fits
  A′ > 0, where both are strength 35. The line that doesn't fit, 29725.817, is
  a blend.
* `A_hfs_levels.csv` gives +0.087 ± 0.034 from the composition. That agrees.

**1093–972: two lines, to F = 3.**

* 28659.387 (I 4670) and 28658.990 (I 4669) are 3A′ = 0.397 apart, so
  **|A′(972)| = 0.132 ± 0.012**. The two strengths, 28 and 35, differ by a
  factor of only 0.8, so the equal intensities cannot fix the sign.
* `A_hfs_levels.csv` gives +0.030 ± 0.012 from the composition, 8σ from this.
  Either the composition value of 972 is wrong, or the doublet is not this
  transition's hfs.
* The lines to F = 2 are expected 1.52 higher, at about 28660.9 (3 → 2,
  strength 35) and 28660.5 (2 → 2, strength 10). No line is recorded between
  28659.387 and 28670. On the strength ratio, 3 → 2 should be about as bright as
  the recorded pair, so it is missing.
