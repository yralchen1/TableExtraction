MERGED LINES OF SUGAR'S LIST - the full account
================================================

Kept by hand.  The short notes in Pr3_lines.xlsx (and so in
final/Pr3_lines_set3.xlsx) point here.  Two kinds of merge are recorded:

A. MERGED IN THE LINE LIST (Pr3_lines.xlsx).  Two of Sugar's lines are one
   line in every set from then on: one row, one wn_key, one position the fit
   uses.  Done with LineClass/Devel/sugar_duplicates_2026-11-20/merge_lines.py,
   which also re-keys line_decisions.csv, kappa_exceptions.txt,
   inflated_unc_lines.txt, line_snr.csv and iter_hfs/IDEN2 (dlv.dat,
   dlv_keys.txt, dlv_shown.txt).  The merged line keeps the identified row,
   with its character and stated uncertainty.  Its wavenumber is the plain
   average of the two.  Its intensity (Iorig, Icor) is the mean of the two
   for two readings of one line, and the sum for two parts of one feature.

B. COMPANIONS (hfs_satellites.txt).  The second line stays in the line list
   with grade hfs, out of LOPT and IDEN2.  make_set3.py merges it into its
   main line in Set 3, adding its intensity.  The fit uses the main line
   alone, held at the head of its pattern (kappa = 1), unless
   kappa_exceptions.txt says otherwise.

Positions are given from the center of gravity (cg) of the transition, the
Ritz value of LOPT's final fit (2026-11-20).  "Peaks" are the maxima of the
calculated hyperfine pattern (A_hfs_levels.csv, hfs_patterns.components)
with each component broadened to the effective line width (make_set3.fwhm_cm:
0.035 A instrumental with the Doppler width).  The scatter of ln(Iobs/Icalc)
of single 1974 lines is 0.90 (robust), so a factor of 2 in intensity
decides nothing alone.

How the cases were found: Devel/sugar_duplicates_2026-11-20/close_pairs.py
(neighbors closer than one line width: 109 pairs, 70 of 1969, 39 of 1974) and
inside_pattern.py (an unidentified line inside an identified neighbor's
broadened pattern, above half its peak height: 8 lines on 2026-11-20).

C. PRINTED COMPONENTS MADE LINES (Pr3_lines.xlsx, 11/21/2026)
=============================================================

Sugar printed these with Intens = 0 as the hyperfine components of a flagged
line; they fit no rung or maximum of that line's pattern
(Contradictions_for_paper.md item 2, cases a and b).  hfs_components.DISMISSED
drops them from hfs_components.csv and the A fit, and each is a line of
Pr3_lines.xlsx (appended rows, like 30879.5492 and 30879.1586 on 10/9/2026).
Sugar printed no intensity: Icor and Iorig are the main line's times the
rung-1 to head strength ratio of the main line's own pattern (the user's
rule).
