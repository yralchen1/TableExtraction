wavelength_calibration.py - the plate calibration of the observed line list
the displacement delta_lambda is in angstrom, positive where Sugar's wavelength is too long;
a positive delta_lambda therefore means his wavenumber is too small, and the
correction to add to it is +delta_lambda * wn^2 * 1e-8.

assignments: iter_hfs\line_classifications.csv, read on wn_key (the wavenumber Sugar published)
model: one constant per group of at least 12 lines, and one over any thinner block that has at least 2

The thin blocks, on trial
-------------------------
A block that cannot raise 12 lines for a group of its own was given one
constant over the whole block and then asked whether that constant is worth
keeping.  The test is the parameter against its own uncertainty: a constant
smaller than its uncertainty has measured nothing and is fixed at zero.  Such
a block trades its constant almost exactly against the energies of the few
levels its lines touch, and keeping it lets that near-degeneracy leak through
the common mode into every other group in the spectrum: with all six kept the
largest eigenvalue of the calibration covariance rises by a factor of 66.
The lines of a fixed block still carry the uncertainty measured here, against
a correction of zero.

the one remaining rank deficiency is the energy zero: every level can be raised by
the same amount without changing a single level difference, so nothing observable
moves along it.  The direction was found from the design matrix itself, and the
largest weight it puts on any calibration parameter is 2.39e-16 against 3.92e-02 on the
levels, so the curve is not touched by it and its uncertainties are not inflated by it.

The covariance matrix is the pseudo-inverse of the weighted normal matrix, formed
once for the whole fit, so every uncertainty below carries the correlation between
the calibration and the level energies, and between one coefficient and the next.
Its largest departure from symmetry before it was symmetrized was 3.30e-12 of its
largest element - rounding, as it must be.  The leverage inflation u/(1-h) is not
used here and cannot distort it: the fit weights each line by that line's own
uncertainty, and the leverage is computed from the solution, never fed back into it.
The calibration block of it, 153 by 153, has eigenvalues from 5.990e-08 to 2.632e-04: all
positive, so it is a proper covariance matrix and any linear combination of the
calibration parameters has a positive variance.

Is a plate smooth?  The polynomial against the staircase
-------------------------------------------------------
Every block is fitted both ways.  chi2 is that block's share of the total; deg is
the degree the price rule selected, and top the highest degree the block may carry.
The smoothness test is the staircase against chi2_top, the best a polynomial can do
there: d_chi2 is what the staircase buys with its d_par extra parameters, and noise
alone would buy d_par +- sqrt(2 d_par), which is what the last column counts.

The degrees were chosen one block at a time, and up to the ceiling of the block:
every degree is tried, not only the degrees up to the first one that fails: a plate
whose error is a symmetric bow gains nothing from a slope and a great deal from
a curve, and a search that stopped at the first disappointment would never find
it.  A degree costs 9.0 in chi2 per parameter it adds, which is 3 sigma each,
and the winner is the degree that beats that price by the widest margin; a block
may carry a parameter only per 12 of its lines.  "pays" below is whether that one
degree covers its own cost, not whether it won: the fitted degree is in the
table above.  The scan is repeated until no block changes its mind, since the
blocks are coupled through the level energies they share.

A calibration error is not noise: at a given wavelength it displaces every line the
same way, so it does not average down over the lines of a level and belongs in a
LOPT run as a systematic uncertainty, not as a widening of the individual line
uncertainties.

The mean over all groups is what the fit says about the wavelength scale as a
whole, and it is not held to zero by anything: the shape of the displacement in
wavenumber, wn^2, is not a shape the level values can imitate, so the network of
Ritz combinations measures it.  It is measured weakly, though, which is what the
uncertainty in this table says; the differences between one part of the curve and
another are much better determined than their common level.

The statistical uncertainty of a corrected wavenumber
-----------------------------------------------------
Once the calibration error is taken out of a line, what is left is Sugar's measuring
scatter, and it is that - not his stated uncertainty, and not the uncertainty adopted
before the curve was known - that belongs in the LOPT input as the uncertainty of a
corrected wavenumber.  The table is over the lines of parameterized blocks only, the
rest having no correction to remove.  "after" is the rms of the residuals of this
fit over the effective number of degrees of freedom of the class, sum(1 - h), which
is what is left of its lines once the fitted level values have taken their share;
the floor of 0.0055 cm-1 is hfs_kappa.FLOOR.

a_A and b_cm1 are the two terms of the class model, u^2 = (a*1e-8*wn^2)^2 + b^2;
adopted is their mean over the class and "after" the rms of the residuals, both
in cm-1.  The two are not the same average - the rms leans on the widest lines of
the class and the mean does not - so the test of the model is the last column,
chi2/dof = sum(r^2/u^2) / sum(1-h), which must be close to 1.

The lines that carry it
-----------------------
mean leverage 0.179 over 4498 lines: that fraction of each residual has already been
absorbed by the fitted level values, and a fit made to wavelength_calibration_points.csv
without allowing for it will report a chi-square low by about as much.  The column
u_eff_A = u_d_lambda_A / (1 - leverage) is the weight to fit by instead.  It is a
weight for an outside fit only: this program never uses it, so it cannot reach the
covariance matrix.
11 lines reach a leverage of 0.999 or more - one of their levels rests on that
line alone, so the fit satisfies them exactly whatever the calibration is; they
measure nothing.
