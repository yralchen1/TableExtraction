# Plan for paper 1: LLM-assisted extraction of the Pr III line lists and their identification with the DREAM transition probabilities

Draft of 2026-11-22, revised twice the same day after the user's reviews.

## 1. Scope

Paper 1 describes stages 1-3 of the ICAMDATA 2026 (Mons) talk, slides 4, 5, 8-10:

1. An AI-assisted system that digitizes large printed line lists.
2. With it, a complete list of the observed Pr III lines of Sugar (1969, 1974).
3. An automated system that assigns observed lines to the energy levels of
   J.-F. Wyart's list, with the transition probabilities (TP) of the DREAM
   database, and validates every new level statistically.

Authors: A. Kramida, M. Pandey, D. Budker, Yu. Ralchenko, R. Ibacache.

Everything done after the DREAM TPs were replaced by the new Cowan-code TP set
belongs to paper 2 and is not described here.

The results at 5f415ef agree with the numbers in slide 9 of the talk (checked
2026-11-22 against `LineClass/line_classifications.csv` at that commit):

- 6673 observed lines (7171 with Sugar's hfs components); 3367 classified in
  print, carrying 3477 classifications.
- With the DREAM TPs: 4553 lines classified, 652 of them multiply, 1588 with
  new identifications.
- All 593 of Wyart's levels supported by observed lines; 59 levels have no DREAM
  transition in range; of the 214 tested levels about 33 are likely spurious;
  50 marked questionable.
- Finding: chance coincidences are frequent; even a level with 10-12
  supporting lines can be spurious with up to about 5% probability.

## 3. Paper 2 as a revision of paper 1 (user, 2026-11-22)

Paper 1's files show paper 1's decisions; paper 2's files show the revised ones.
`Pr3_lines.xlsx` (the pipeline's input; not published, but in the public
repositories) changes as little as possible in both papers, and no row is
inserted or deleted in either: paper 2's codes depend on its copy. The Notes of
the two papers may differ but should not contradict each other; where a revision
makes them differ, a column **Notes2** says so. In paper 1, paper 2 is cited as
"to be published".

Revision codes (a different uncertainty inflation is not a revision):

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A2 | A line Sugar printed that was missing from paper 1's line list; added in paper 2 |
| R2 | Revised in paper 2: Sugar's intensity, character or classification |
| L2 | Sugar's printed hfs component made a line in paper 2 |
| M2 | Merged with another line in paper 2 |
| C2 | Identified as an hfs companion in paper 2 |

Codes are joined with a comma (`R2,C2`). Paper 1's files carry the codes only;
the legend goes into the README and the supplement. Paper 2's files carry each
code with a short explanation. The codes were generated by comparing the two
papers' files, reviewed by the user, and are frozen when paper 1 is submitted;
later revisions are recorded in paper 2 only. Changes to the identifications
themselves (Sugar's classifications accepted in one paper and not in the other:
176 one way, 14 the other, on 2026-11-22) are not coded here; they go into paper
2's final line list as its coded notes.
