Note (1): The identifier used throughout this work and in the line list
    (Supplementary Data 3), 059003.nnnnnn (059 = Pr, 003 = the third spectrum).
Note (2): NIST ASD notation, as in NIST ASD and in Wyart's list: subshells and
    parent terms in parentheses are separated by periods; the J of a parent term
    is given in angle brackets, e.g., 4f2.(3H<4>).5f.
Note (3): LS, JK (e.g., 2[5]*) or J1j (e.g., (4,1/2)) coupling, as in NIST ASD;
    an asterisk marks odd parity. An asterisk alone, or a blank, means that no
    term is assigned.
Note (4): The energy of Wyart's list (W99), printed with its own number of
    decimal places. For the levels known before (Ref M78), Wyart adopted the
    energies of M78, except where Ref begins with W99. See also Rem.
Note (5): Optimized with the program LOPT (Kramida 2011, Comput. Phys. Commun.
    182, 419) from the lines classified in this work (Supplementary Data 3),
    weighted by the uncertainties of the observed wavenumbers; the components of
    a blended line share it in proportion to their calculated intensities.
Note (6): The larger of LOPT's two uncertainty estimates D1 and D2; zero for the
    ground level, whose energy is fixed.
Note (7): Codes as follows:
      M78 = W. C. Martin, R. Zalubas, and L. Hagan, Atomic Energy Levels -
            The Rare-Earth Elements, Nat. Stand. Ref. Data Ser., NSRDS-NBS 60
            (Nat. Bur. Stand., U.S., 1978); DOI:10.6028/NBS.NSRDS.60
      L16 = H. Li, X.-Y. Kuang, and Y. Y. Yeung, J. Lumin. 170, Part 2,
            380-386 (2016); DOI:10.1016/j.jlumin.2015.07.036
      W99 = J.-F. Wyart, List of experimental energy levels of Pr III,
            private communication to P. Palmeri, unpublished (1999)
    When two codes are given, the first is the source of the energy and the
    second the source of the NIST ASD percentage composition (Perc to Term2) or,
    for L16, which gives no composition, of the term label. A single code is the
    source of both the energy and the NIST ASD composition, if any.
Note (8): Codes as follows:
      N  = level new in Wyart's list, absent from NIST ASD;
      ?  = questionable in Wyart's list;
      N? = both.
Note (9): Observed lines classified in this work as transitions from or to the
    level: Nold were classified before (Sugar 1969, 1974), Nnew are new
    identifications.
Note (10): Given only for the tested levels, which are those for which the newly
    identified supporting lines are more numerous than old ones.
Note (11): A tested level is marked Q when Pspur >= 0.1, unless at least 90% of
    the predicted intensity missing from its ten strongest predicted transitions
    falls at positions hidden by stronger observed lines (unresolved from them,
    or under the wing of a much stronger line) and the probability from its
    energy shift alone is below 0.1.
Note (12): Codes as follows (paper 2: A. Kramida, to be published):
      R2 = energy or designation revised in paper 2;
      D2 = level discarded in paper 2.
Note (13): Remarks as follows:
      a = E_W99 raised by 0.05 cm-1 from Wyart's 141328.80 so that his
          classification is compatible with the observed line;
      b = Wyart's 107233.20, corrected by Wyart to 107223.20 (as reported
          by P. Palmeri, see P26 in Note 15);
      c = absent from the surviving copies of Wyart's list; from P26 (Note
          15), whose levels were all communicated by Wyart;
      d = Wyart's energy replaces that of M78 (46673.78 for 059003.000184,
          88220.2 for 059003.000304), which he rejected.
Note (14): Percentage composition as given in NIST ASD (from the source named
    second in Ref, or from M78): Perc is the percentage of the leading
    component, the one named by Config and Term, or by Term1 where Term is not
    given; Or = "or" means that Perc2 is the percentage of the same component in
    another coupling scheme, named by Conf2 and Term2, not of a second
    component. A blank Conf2 means the configuration of Config; a parent term in
    parentheses at the start of Term2 replaces the parent term of Config.
Note (15): P26 = P. Palmeri, Experimental and least-squares-fitted Pr III energy
    levels used in the DREAM database, private communication to A. Kramida,
    unpublished (March 2, 2026): the eigenvectors of the
    Hartree-Fock-relativistic calculation behind the transition probabilities of
    the DREAM database. Given for every level: the three LS components P26
    lists, in its order and with its percentages, attached to the energy that
    P26 gives them (see Note 16), converted to NIST ASD notation. P26 does not
    distinguish the repeated terms of 4f3; here they carry the Nielsen-Koster
    index used in NIST ASD (e.g., 2F2*), found by matching each component with
    the eigenvector of the same level in a Cowan-code calculation (paper 2). The
    calculation gives equal weights to the two 2F terms in 059003.000044, which
    are therefore left without index.
Note (16): Codes as follows:
      P1 = the configuration P26 assigns differs from Config;
      P2 = same configuration, but the largest P26 component is not the
           LS state named by the designation or by the NIST ASD
           composition (Term1, or Conf2 and Term2 after "or"); for JK
           and J1j designations, only the parent term is compared;
      P3 = the P26 compositions of this level and of another level of the
           same J and parity appear interchanged; the pairs are
           059003.000346 and 059003.000555;
           059003.000409 and 059003.000413;
           059003.000513 and 059003.000514.
